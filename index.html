Did you know? There was a time foxes and cats playing with rabbits. All eating fresh grass and vegetables that flourish on the ground. But Firth got angry to the Prince of Rabbits. So they cursed the foxes and cats, alongside with the all other hunters known to rabbits. So much to count, hrair. Making all of them prey on their once friend. Now all the rabbits shall run and run, hide and hide from all elil out there. Will there be any time a prince with thousand enemies, El-ahrairah, slay the Firth and bring back the peace.
Did you know? Rabbits only count to four. Anything more than that is hrair. So one thousand elil and five elil feel about the same.
Did you know? When a rabbit is really happy, it jumps and twists in the air. It is called a binky. [your own fun fact can go here]
Did you know? A rabbit's teeth never stop growing, which is why they chew on everything. Please keep the stories away from them.
Did you know? Rabbits can see almost all the way around their heads, which is how they spot every elil coming.
