Good Girl Rena is Loved by Her Big-Sis Hatsune

With the morning shine leaking through the curtains I, Rena, get into a half-asleep half-awake daze. I have slept with my dear Hatsune. The girl with brown eyes, long pink hair, and a sweet voice. It was our first day of living together, but I insisted on sleeping together. And, she was also very positive about it. I slept like babies this night, I have never slept this good in my life.

Well, the dear girl of me is still in the bed with me, so I hug the dear girl in my arms. She is soft and nice smelling, I squeeze her more to fill up my Hatsune energy. But... isn't she a bit too soft? My Hatsune isn't a plump girl. So why am I able to squeeze her like a sponge, as if she has no bones in her body? Caressing her body with my hand, I feel around of her but cannot feel the curves of her hips or waist. Carrying my hand upwards to feel her long graceful neck... There is no neck! And, I don't feel her silky pink hair nor can find her small head!! With the dread, as if my guts in knots, I jump out of the bed and look at my beloved 'Hatsune', but there is no Hatsune at all! Just a pillow which is Hatsune-scented. Where did my dear girlfriend has go? No way, right? No way she has left me without saying anything... uuuuu Hatsune... (Poor Rena... she is too silly to mistake a pillow to her GF)

As I was planning about my new career as 'heart-broken poet', I hear a very soothing humming. It comes from the outside of this room. With a dazed mind, my legs moves before I think, I do have limbs that could move on their own as if they have their own minds. The humming itself is out of tune, or a tuneless tune, but somehow it feels like home? Even though, I am already at home. The boiling water and the chopping of vegetables on cutting board also adds a nice flavor to the over all humming, like a mini lullaby created by simple instruments. And... And.... Lo and behold! I cannot believe in my eyes, an Angel has descended from heaven into my kitchen to cook food for me! Wohoo, I am the luckiest girl in the world! (What about your career of heartbroken poet now... No? damn...)

"The breakfast will be ready in a moment. Rena, go and wash your hands and wait at the table like a 'good-girl', OK?" The angel with her radiant smile, looks at me and reminds me to wash my hands properly, as the good girl I am. I zoom to the bathroom, wash my hands properly without wasting any water. I don't know, but I am brimming with a yearning for to be called 'good-girl' by Hatsune, my angel. There is some changes from yesterday, as she has looked so fragile and broken, to the point I have made a vow of protecting her for my life, but she gives a different vibe, like a big sis... But! I actually like an older sister better, I want to be her sweet and cute little sister. And get tons of hugs and head-pats while called as 'good-girl'. (Btw the 'good girl' also works as verb, like getting petted, a sweet child verb... But this girl Rena, aren't a young adult in twenties?)

Sitting on the table, waiting diligently as the good girl I am, Hatsune comes with the breakfast. The smile on her sweet and small face has never been this bright before. But that smile fills something in me, like drinking sweet cocktail, with the slight dizziness and happiness it gives. It is simply mesmerizing. I wonder if her better constitution comes from staying with me.

(I also wonder about why? Is staying with silly girls a good thing... Did Hatsune take advantage of Rena at night?)

Hatsune, my angel, gives me a 'good-girl' on the head which I savor with the every strand of my hair. I have earned this 'good-girl', right? She says nothing, but I can see in her clear jade like green eyes, she is giving me this 'good-girl' because I washed my hands and waited properly!

(Since when Hatsune has green eyes... This silly Rena somehow forgot that dark colored jades exists, and made such an absurd comment?)

The humming of her soothes me. I pop a egg-roll into my mouth, it is sweet and soft, I think she has made it with a bit of cream to make it. But there is something odd of its texture, not in a bad way though. As if I am getting 'good-girl' inside of mouth. What is this, the love of Hatsune? While thinking about this, I realize the fatal mistake I have made, how much of a fool I am to let my guard down! On the trench made of egg rolls, I see the green alien soldiers. I have been played out by an angel. How cruel the life is... My beloved Hatsune attacking me with the worst kind of soldiers that exists. I pitifully look at Hatsune, pouting. Hmm... for some reason I feel a bit shorter than I am used to? No, I guess Hatsune looks much taller since she has gained her vigor again?

(Well broccoli isn't tasty as a kid... But I do like them. I have grown old... sigh)

"No, don't even try to make a negotiation about it, Rena-chan. I am not going to make a lie about: 'You need to eat broccoli to grow up.' since you don't need it. I am not a bad older sister who tell lies." Hatsune makes her first point in a strict tone, then starts to smile again, "But if you eat broccoli which I have made for you, with my love for you, even though you hate broccoli, you are a good girl who eats whatever you big-sis cooks. And, this would make me really happy."

(What kind of manipulation is this!???)

What an angel, yes! Hatsune never lies to me. How can I say no to her, now. As she educates me in her nice voice meanwhile humming this soothing tune. I'd even eat poison if it was cooked by the love of my angel. "Bring them on. I will eat any green alien and make them nourishment for myself!" how is she humming while talking? Is she doing a ventriloquism?

While fighting with the nemesis of humanity, I steak a glance of Hatsune, but... Huh? I am sure she looked a bit taller than she used to be, but isn't she a bit too tall now, much bigger than me. Hmm... While thinking about this, the fork piercing into a green alien, falls from my hand. When I pick it up again, it feels a bit awkward to hold. Like it also has grown a bit more. And that's not the only problem, the plate, cup, the table, the chair I sit on, the very room I eat the food... Everything has grown more? Maybe I am drunk... with the love of Hatsune! Yes, that should be it. While thinking about this stuff, the humming rhythm changes a little, the melody is more complex and it is a bit more fast paced. But it is nice to hear! So I am happy hear it.

(This girl is impossible...)

"I'm proud of you, my dear little Rena, who eats broccoli even though she hates them, for her big sister's sake. You would do anything I ask of you, right? Since, you love me, your big-sis, so much right?"

"Yes I love Hatsune so sooo much, this much. (Opening her arms wide to show the amount of love she has, is she really an adult?) I would do anything my dear Big-sis!" I say without any hesitation. At this point the table is so big that only my head and shoulders peaks up? Well whatever, the humming of Hatsune intensifies once more. One sec... she isn't humming, it sounds like human but no. Oh! There is a music box on the table, how didn't I realize it was here? Did I had such a thing at my house? Maybe it is something Hatsune has brought with herself.

Well, it doesn't matter. I go ahead and eat the green alien. Thinking about it now, it doesn't taste bad that much. And it tastes nostalgic... as if something I have eaten many times as a kid. Something that I have been scolded? Yes! I remember, this is the taste of play-dough. The texture and the taste matches. Who would've know broccoli tastes like green play-dough. Then I go ahead and eat one of the egg roles again. Huh? They also taste and feel like yellow play-dough! Well, whatever.

My big-sis, angel, play-mate, picks me up, caressing my cheek with her big index finger. "Aww, my sweet doll Rena would do anything her Big-sis asks of her. How much of a good-girl you are. Here, 'good-girl' 'good-girl' (headpat)"

Uhh... now I understand. I was her doll to begin with right? Yes, it must be it. She made me her sister to play, Changed my diapers and feeding me. I, Rena the Doll, feel the utmost ecstasy and fulfillment. With the music box playing in an endless loop, Hatsune gives me 'good-girl'. How happy I am!

"Puhuhu, you have really become my sweet sister-doll, I bet your sisters would also love you. It is time to meet with the other doll-family members. You would be a good sweet doll-sister, right?"

I can't help but add... If you were to open the music box and look at the hidden mechanism, it consists two bands which I leave it to your imagination to think what the materials are. Those bands are similar to a human's vocal cords. The rotation of the spring clock let the air get into the box and changes the stretch of the bands, effectively simulating the pitch change. And the buzz-air gets into a compartment, which looks very similar to a nasal cavity, making the awkward humming sound. It is a mystery how the clock spring powers this system for a long time... The tune change done with quick replacement of different music rolls... but the mechanism of this replacement seems to react some signal, as there are some sensors. Growing underneath the music box, similar to sprouts on the back of a cockroach. What do they sense within the air, to change the tune roll... I also wonder. This absurd instrument, with archaic witch language, is called as 'doll-maker'.
